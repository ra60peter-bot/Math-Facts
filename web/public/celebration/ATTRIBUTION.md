# Celebration artwork

Party face (U+1F973), laughing face (U+1F606), and sunglasses face (U+1F60E)
are unmodified SVG artwork from [Blobmoji](https://github.com/C1710/blobmoji),
based on Google's Noto Emoji and maintained by C1710 and Blobmoji contributors.

Source: https://github.com/C1710/blobmoji/tree/main/svg
